## Research interests
---
Lynsay investigates human behavior in the context of cybersecurity, immersive experiences, and gaming, specialising in how users navigate secure interactions within emerging technologies. Her work leverages high-fidelity methods—including eye-tracking, applied games, and XR—to build a safer, more accessible Metaverse. By bridging the gap between human psychology and AI/Machine Learning, Lynsay develops proactive solutions for detecting romance fraud and enhancing digital safety for all users.

Lynsay founded the Human-Centred Emerging Technologies Lab - <a href="https://lynsay.github.io/HCET-Lab/">https://lynsay.github.io/HCET-Lab/</a>.
